# Tandoori Chicken Wraps with Coriander-Mint Chutney

## Description

Smoky, yogurt-marinated tandoori chicken wrapped in warm naan with a fresh coriander-mint chutney and tangy pickled onions. Inspired by North Indian street food, it's perfect for groups since everyone can build their own wrap.

### Ingredients:

**Tandoori chicken**
- [ ] 2 lbs boneless chicken thighs, cut into strips
- [ ] 1 cup plain yogurt
- [ ] 2 tbsp tandoori masala
- [ ] 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- [ ] 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- [ ] 1 tbsp lemon juice
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 2 tbsp oil

**Coriander-mint chutney**
- [ ] 1 cup fresh coriander (cilantro) leaves
- [ ] 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves
- [ ] 1 green chili
- [ ] 2 tbsp plain yogurt
- [ ] 1 tbsp lemon juice
- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt

**Pickled onions**
- [ ] 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1/2 cup white vinegar
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt

**To assemble**
- [ ] 6 naan or rotis
- [ ] 2 cups shredded lettuce
- [ ] 1 cucumber, sliced

### Steps for preparation:

1. Mix the yogurt, tandoori masala, chili powder, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice and salt in a bowl. Add the chicken, coat well, cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
2. While the chicken marinates, place the sliced onion in a jar. Stir together the vinegar, sugar and salt, pour it over the onions and set aside for at least 30 minutes.
3. Blend the coriander, mint, green chili, yogurt, lemon juice and salt until smooth. Add a splash of water if it's too thick.
4. Heat the oil in a large pan or grill pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 6-8 minutes per side until slightly charred and cooked through.
5. Warm the naan in a dry pan or oven for 1-2 minutes.
6. Set out the chicken, chutney, pickled onions, lettuce and cucumber so everyone can build their own wrap.
7. To assemble: spread chutney on the naan, add lettuce, chicken, cucumber and pickled onions, then roll it up.

### Notes:

- Swap the chicken for paneer to make it vegetarian.
- The pickled onions and chutney can be made a day ahead.
- Use regular paprika if you can't find Kashmiri chili powder.
