# Chicken Burrito Bowl

## Description

A filling, customizable bowl of cilantro-lime rice, black beans, grilled chicken and fresh toppings. Inspired by the burrito spots around Berkeley's campus, it's a quick go-to lunch between classes.

### Ingredients:

**Cilantro-lime rice**
- [ ] 1 cup white rice
- [ ] 2 cups water
- [ ] 1 lime, juiced
- [ ] 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt

**Chicken**
- [ ] 1 lb boneless chicken breast or thighs
- [ ] 1 tsp chili powder
- [ ] 1 tsp cumin
- [ ] 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tbsp oil

**Toppings**
- [ ] 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 1 cup corn
- [ ] 1 cup pico de gallo or salsa
- [ ] 1 avocado, sliced
- [ ] 1 cup shredded lettuce
- [ ] 1/2 cup shredded cheese
- [ ] Sour cream (optional)

### Steps for preparation:

1. Rinse the rice, then cook it with the water and salt in a covered pot over low heat for 15-18 minutes until the water is absorbed.
2. Fluff the rice with a fork and stir in the lime juice and cilantro.
3. Rub the chicken with chili powder, cumin, garlic powder and salt.
4. Heat the oil in a pan over medium-high heat and cook the chicken for 5-6 minutes per side until cooked through. Rest it for 5 minutes, then slice.
5. Warm the black beans and corn in a small pot for 3-4 minutes.
6. Build each bowl: rice first, then beans, corn, chicken, lettuce, pico de gallo, cheese and avocado.
7. Top with sour cream and an extra squeeze of lime if you like.

### Notes:

- Swap the chicken for tofu or extra beans to make it vegetarian.
- The rice, beans and chicken keep well in the fridge for 3-4 days for meal prep.
